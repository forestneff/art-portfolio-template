The Vision

Bridging the gap between the digital void and physical permanence.

My work explores the fragility of memory in the digital age. By combining classical sculpting techniques with generative algorithms, I create artifacts that exist in a liminal space—simultaneously ancient and futuristic. This portfolio serves as a living archive of that exploration.
